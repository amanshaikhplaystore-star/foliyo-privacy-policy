# Foliyo Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 12 October 2026**

Foliyo (package name `com.foliyo.app`) helps you create resumes and cover letters. This policy explains what information the Android app handles and when it is shared.

## Information stored on your device

Foliyo does not require an account. Resume and cover-letter content, saved profile details, imported content, photos you select, app preferences, and AI provider settings are stored in the app's local storage on your device. Foliyo does not operate a server that receives or synchronizes your documents.

Android device backup may include some Foliyo app data, depending on your Android version and backup settings. Saved AI provider credentials are excluded from app backup and are encrypted using Android Keystore on your device. Uninstalling Foliyo removes its local app data, except for anything retained separately in a device backup or files you exported.

## AI features and third-party providers

If you choose to use an AI feature, the text needed to fulfill your request (which may include resume, profile, or cover-letter information you supplied) is sent to the AI provider selected in the app:

- **Built-in Foliyo AI:** Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes the request under its applicable terms and privacy disclosures.
- **Your own provider:** If you add a provider and API key, requests are sent directly from the app to that provider, such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or an OpenAI-compatible service you configure. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply. For a self-hosted or local provider, the request goes to the server address you entered.

AI use is optional. Do not include information in an AI request that you do not want that provider to process. Foliyo does not receive a copy of these requests through a Foliyo-operated server. Provider API keys are stored encrypted on your device and sent to the selected provider only as needed to authenticate requests.

## Files, exports, and sharing

When you import a file, Android's file picker lets you select it for Foliyo to read. Foliyo processes imported content on your device unless you choose an AI feature, in which case relevant text may be sent as described above. PDFs you create are saved or shared only when you choose an export, save, print, or share action. The destination app or storage provider then handles the file under its own policies.

## Information not collected by Foliyo

The current app does not require account or contact details, and does not include Foliyo-operated analytics, crash reporting, or advertising. Google Play and Android may process information as part of installing, updating, backing up, or running the app under their own privacy terms. Google services used for built-in AI may process technical information needed to provide and protect that service.

## Children's privacy

Foliyo is a general-purpose resume and cover-letter tool and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Foliyo-operated service.

## Your choices and data deletion

You can avoid AI processing by not using AI features. You can remove documents, profiles, and provider settings in the app. You can also clear Foliyo's app data or uninstall the app in Android settings. Data already sent to an AI provider, exported, shared, or retained in device backups may need to be deleted separately with that provider or from the relevant destination.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version should be available at the privacy-policy URL linked from the Google Play listing.
